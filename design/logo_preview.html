Logo Concepts — Offline Mesh Messaging

Three Directions

01 — Pulse
A single node broadcasting outward through two concentric arcs. The horseshoe shape avoids the WiFi cliché — this is not a router, it's a peer.
#1C1C1E White
Elements: 1 circle (center node) + 2 arcs (270° sweep, open at bottom)
Stroke: round linecap, 3.5px inner / 2.5px outer
Feel: Radar. Broadcasting. Calm authority.
02 — Triad
Three equal peers forming a closed triangle. No hierarchy, no center — pure mesh. The most honest diagram of decentralized communication.
#007AFF White
Elements: 3 circles (r=5.5) + 3 lines at 60% opacity
Triangle: equilateral, circumradius=22, one vertex up
Feel: Democratic. Structured. Network-native.
03 — Relay
A hub node relaying to three peers — the Y-shape captures the mesh routing mechanic. One device receives and forwards. Structural, immediate, functional.
#F2F2F7 #1C1C1E
Elements: 1 hub (r=7.5) + 3 nodes (r=5) + 3 spokes
Spokes: 120° intervals, 28px length from hub
Feel: Mechanical. Purposeful. Action-oriented.

Animation Concepts

01 — Pulse

On launch: the center dot fades in first (300ms, spring), then the inner arc draws itself clockwise (400ms ease-out), then the outer arc follows with a 150ms delay. On message received: the outer arc briefly pulses outward by 2px and fades back. Keyframe: scale 1.0 → 1.05 → 1.0, opacity 0.45 → 0.8 → 0.45.

02 — Triad

On launch: the three nodes pop in sequentially (top → bottom-left → bottom-right, 80ms apart, spring animation). The connecting lines draw afterward, each from node to node (200ms stroke-dashoffset animation). On connection established: a brief flash travels along one edge — a small highlight dot sliding along the line stroke.

03 — Relay

On launch: hub scales in (spring), then spokes extend from center outward simultaneously (300ms ease-out, transform-origin: center hub), then outer nodes pop in at the tips. On message relay: a dot travels from one outer node → hub → another outer node, easing in and out. Duration 600ms.
